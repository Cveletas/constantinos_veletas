About Me

I work at the intersection of analysis and hardware. My strongest experience has come from taking propulsion systems from requirements and first-principles engineering through CAD, fabrication, assembly, verification, and test. I thrive in environments where I am not already the expert and can learn new trades on the fly, using each new challenge to become a more flexible engineer. I am especially interested in propulsion hardware, fluid-system design, ignition mechanisms, and ground-support testing.

Relevant Projects

Additively Manufactured Rocket Engine Injector

CU Sounding Rocket Lab · June 2025–present · in development

A design effort for SRL’s first additively manufactured liquid rocket engine injector, taken from initial requirements through injector sizing and manufacturing readiness review.

  • Combined high-pressure systems, injector orifice design, and fluid dynamics to translate engine requirements into injector geometry.
  • Applied additive-manufacturing constraints and design-for-manufacturing considerations to the component architecture.
  • Used FEM analysis alongside analytical engineering work to evaluate the design before review.
  • Carried the design through manufacturing readiness review (MRR) toward a manufacturable, test-ready component.

Diablo Liquid Engine Test Stand

CU Sounding Rocket Lab · June 2025–present · assembled and tested

Engine test infrastructure built and integrated for SRL’s liquid-engine test program, with work spanning hardware fabrication, fluid compatibility, subsystem integration, verification, and controls.

  • Manufactured test-stand components and prepared subassemblies for LOX compatibility.
  • Assembled individual subsystems and integrated them into the overall test stand.
  • Conducted low-pressure and hydrostatic testing before firing operations.
  • Learned NI LabVIEW to control the stand during static-fire operations.

Scorpion & Taipan Liquid Engines

CU Sounding Rocket Lab · structural analysis, manufacturing & verification · in development

Structural, manufacturing, and verification work across the Scorpion and Taipan liquid-engine programs.

  • Performed structural margin calculations for engine components to evaluate their design adequacy.
  • Manufactured a majority of engine components using mills and lathes, translating designs into hardware.
  • Manufactured composite liners as part of the engine hardware build.
  • Conducted hydrostatic testing to verify the structures against the analysis.

Liquid Engine Ignition System

CU Sounding Rocket Lab · June 2025–present · iteratively tested

An ignition-system development effort supporting SRL’s current liquid rocket engines, combining solid-propellant chemistry, ballistics, gas dynamics, modeling, and repeated physical testing.

  • Combined solid-propellant chemistry, ballistic behavior, and gas dynamics to develop the ignition approach.
  • Designed the ignition system for SRL’s current liquid rocket engines.
  • Compared modeled behavior with physical results and iterated the design between tests.
  • Validated the system through multiple successful static fires.
